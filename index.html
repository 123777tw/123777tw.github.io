The above is a typical debugging page demo. But it's a little different as it's multilingual. It should be noted that English is always used first, because these texts are directly quoted from GitHub Pages guide manual written in English. And then there are other languages that we translated. If you are a good translator and find that there is something defective with translation, please feel free to propose a revised version.

与上面的样例不同，您正在阅读的这些内容最先使用的是中文。因为它是我写的，我更加擅长中文。同样地，如果您对其他语言的翻译有更好的见解（包括英语），随时提出您的修改版本。

Different from the above demo, the content you are reading is written in Chinese first. Because I wrote it, I am better at Chinese. Similarly, if you have a better understanding of translation in other languages (including English), feel free to put forward your revised version.

翻译成更多的语言，只是为了得到更多人的理解，而没有其他的原因。如果这里没有您熟悉的语言，很遗憾，是我们的能力不足。如果您愿意、并且有能力弥补这个遗憾，我们会非常高兴。同样地，我们的其他项目，也在致力于让更多的人理解。它不仅仅体现在更加详细的多语言注释，同时在设计上也会遵循“最小功耗原则（the principle of Least Power）”。

Translation into more languages, just to get more people's understanding, and there is no other reason. If you are not familiar with any language here, it is a pity that our ability is insufficient. If you are willing and able to make up for this regret, we will be very happy. Similarly, our other projects are also working to make more people understand. It is not only reflected in more detailed multi language annotations, but also follows "The Principle of Least Power" in design.

也就是说，我们正在尽可能地降低我们的软件项目的参与门槛。一个大家几乎都了解的现象，就是有很多盈利性质的组织，在基于一些开放源代码的程序给予有偿服务，甚至即使有些开放源代码协议是苛刻的GPL。造成这一现象的一个最大的原因就是，对于某些人，即使拥有了所有的源代码，可能仍然不能理解它们所代表的含义。“自由软件基金会（Free Software Foundation）”从一个角度限制了自由软件的再垄断行为，但我们致力于从另外一个角度消除再垄断的意义，抑或是仅仅略微提高了再垄断的门槛。

That is to say, we are trying to reduce the threshold of our software project participation as much as possible. Almost everyone knows that there are many profitable organizations that offer paid services based on some open source programs, even though some open source licenses are harsh GPL. One of the biggest reasons for this phenomenon is that for some people, even if they have all the source code, they may still not understand what they mean. The "free software foundation" restricts the re-monopoly of free software from one point of view, but we are committed to eliminating the meaning of re-monopoly from another point of view, or only slightly raising the threshold of re-monopoly.

关于未来软件的发展，是开源更有力量，还是利益驱使更加有效，让我们拭目以待。讨论区欢迎任何不同的观点。

As for the future development of software, whether open source is more powerful or profit driven is more effective remains to be seen. Any different point of view is welcome in Discussions.

我们远离GitHub了一段时间，但我们依然在那里

We've been away from GitHub for a while, but we're still there

这中间发生了很多事情，这包括全世界的格局正在“缓慢”发生的变化，也包括我们遇到的“Access to this site has been restricted”。但总的来说，我们的初心没有变化：我们会尝试一些多数人很少尝试的事情，并致力于让更多人“能够”加入其中，同时不在乎所谓的“商业利益”。如果说“离开”是一个相对的动作，那么变量应该不是我们。

Many things have happened in between, including the "gradual" changes in the global landscape and the "Access to this site has been restricted" we have encountered. But overall, our original intention has not changed: We will try things that most people rarely try, and strive to make more people "able" to join in without caring about so-called "commercial interests". If 'leaving' is a relative action, then the variable should not be us.

不过好消息是，我们回来了，即使我们面临的挑战仍未消失：巨大的AI发展浪潮。当我们重新审视这个世界以后，我们发现之前列出的那些“新闻”，从某种意义上已经不再“那么地”重要——至少我们现在觉得它们已经不需要放在这个首页的下方。

But the good news is that we are back, even though the challenges we face have not disappeared: a huge wave of AI development. When we re-examine the world, we find that the "news" that was previously listed is no longer "so" important in a sense - at least we now feel that they no longer need to be placed at the bottom of this homepage.

这里最应该放置的是我们的态度，而现在，我们会放置更多有关AI的态度。在AI兴起之前，人类在曾在方方面面做出过很多努力，AI的兴起使得其中一些努力变得不再那么有意义，同时又使得另一些努力变得迫在眉睫。我们认为，前者实际上包括了大量“软件商业垄断”，尤其是基于功能相对简单的软件，借助AI甚至可以轻而易举地复刻它们；而后者则包括“保证软件商业垄断”，具体包括各种软件加密手段等等，尤其是功能相对复杂、无法借助AI快速复刻的软件。人类的趋利性没有发生变化，但AI的发展从其他维度改变了软件商业化的规则。

The most appropriate things to place here is our attitude, and now we will place more attitudes towards AI. Before the rise of AI, humans had made many efforts in various aspects. The rise of AI made some of these efforts less meaningful, while also making some others urgent. We believe that the former actually includes a large number of "software business monopolies", especially software based on relatively simple functions, which can even be easily replicated with the help of AI; The latter includes "ensuring software business monopoly", specifically including various software encryption methods, especially software with relatively complex functions that cannot be quickly replicated by AI. The human tendency towards profit has not changed, but the development of AI has changed the rules of software commercialization from other dimensions.

所以我们移除了那些“新闻”，尤其是大多数看起来并没有AI参与的。AI的发展相对于过去的那些事情将会非常迅速，如果还要一条一条罗列它们，将会使人应接不暇（当然，特定的人群永远可以在评论区的特定位置讨论特定的某件事）。所以，我们现在将注意力从特定的人类活动转向了AI的发展趋势，这也会使我们事半功倍。

So we removed those 'news', especially most of them that didn't seem to involve AI. The development of AI will be very rapid compared to the past, and listing them one by one will be overwhelming (of course, specific groups of people can always discuss specific things in specific positions in the comment section). So, we are now shifting our focus from specific human activities to the development trend of AI, which will also make us more efficient.
